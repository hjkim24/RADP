\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

Petals~\cite{borzunov2023petals,borzunov2023distributed}
demonstrated collaborative LLM inference on consumer hardware
with greedy placement and swarm-redundancy recovery, but its
spare-memory assumption --- redundant copies held on consumer GPUs
an order of magnitude beyond a 4\,GB Nano --- excludes that regime.
EdgeShard~\cite{zhang2024edgeshard} splits placement into two
algorithms (latency Algorithm~1, throughput Algorithm~2) each
reported against its own objective, so the relative behaviour
of the two modes under concurrent load is not measured.
Jupiter~\cite{ye2025jupiter} pushes the throughput-optimal end
with a single max-min DP (Eq.~1) plus TBT-SLO constraints on
top of intra-sequence pipeline parallelism; the placement DP
itself is the algorithmic ancestor of our recurrence
(Eq.~\ref{eq:dp} at $\alpha{=}1$ without recovery or
$\gamma_{\mathrm{hop}}$). Neither system provides recovery,
models per-hop framing/GIL overhead, or cross-evaluates the two
placement modes --- the gap the 24-cell matrix in
\S\ref{sec:eval:matrix} fills. Helix~\cite{mei2025helix}
measures greedy placement's 2.38$\times$ tax on heterogeneous
GPU fleets via max-flow, motivating DP-based approaches.
Dai et al.~\cite{dai2025joint} jointly optimise device placement
and model partitioning for cooperative DNN inference on
heterogeneous edge, maximising throughput through a device
pipeline --- but for one-shot DNNs, without the per-token TBT
constraint, KV-cache state, or recovery that autoregressive
decode demands. EdgeLLM~\cite{xu2025edgellm} instead accelerates
LLM inference through speculative decoding on a \emph{single}
device, orthogonal to multi-device placement.
Fault tolerance itself has been addressed on the \emph{training}
side: FTPipeHD~\cite{chen2024ftpipehd} sustains pipeline-parallel
training across heterogeneous edge devices by periodically
replicating weights (to a neighbour and to the central node)
and, on a failure, re-partitioning the model over the survivors
and re-fetching the missing weights before resuming. Its recovery
unit is model weights and its budget is a training batch
(seconds) --- neither transfers to decode, where recovery must
fit within a per-token TBT bound and also restore the in-flight
KV cache.
\sys{} yields EdgeShard's and Jupiter's modes from the same DP
via $\alpha$, co-optimises recovery through Eq.~\ref{eq:mem},
and adds fault tolerance at the 4\,GB-per-peer frontier by
rerouting a failed stage onto a pre-staged single backup within
a per-token budget rather than re-partitioning.
