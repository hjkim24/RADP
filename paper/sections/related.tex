\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

Petals~\cite{borzunov2023petals,borzunov2023distributed}
demonstrated collaborative LLM inference on consumer hardware with
greedy placement and swarm-redundancy-based recovery, but its
16+\,GB peer assumption excludes the 4\,GB Nano regime \sys{}
targets. EdgeShard~\cite{zhang2024edgeshard} introduces a DP-based
latency-optimal placement --- recoverable as our $\alpha{=}0$
boundary (Eq.~\ref{eq:rank}) --- but assumes a redundant copy of
each shard exists somewhere on the cluster; its recovery model
is implicit and pays no memory cost at placement time.
Jupiter~\cite{ye2025jupiter} pushes the throughput-optimal end
($\alpha{=}1$) with explicit TBT-SLO constraints, but offers no
recovery mechanism and no chain forwarding for high $C$.
Helix~\cite{mei2025helix} measures greedy placement's
2.38$\times$ throughput tax on heterogeneous GPU fleets via a
max-flow formulation, motivating DP-based approaches more
generally. \sys{} unifies these threads: the same DP yields
EdgeShard's or Jupiter's placement via the $\alpha$ knob, and the
asynchronous mirror cache plus chain-aware recovery add fault
tolerance neither prior system provides at the 4\,GB-per-peer
edge frontier.
